Why bother now

"I'm young, I'm fine"
is doing a lot of lifting

Heart disease is a slow build, not a switch that flips at 50. The 2010s–2020s decade of your life is where the baseline gets set.

Fact check

Plaque starts early

Fatty streaks in arteries can appear in the teens and twenties. What you're really doing now is choosing the slope of the line, not starting it later.

Silent

High blood pressure has no symptoms

Most young adults with elevated blood pressure have no idea. A 60-second check — free on most campuses — tells you where you stand.
