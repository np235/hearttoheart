Challenge · Beginner · 28 days

The Reset

One habit a week, stacked. By day 28 you're doing all four without thinking about it. No equipment, no cost, no all-or-nothing energy — miss a day, tick the next one.

Move

10 minutes of intentional movement every day. Brisk walk, dance in your room, cycle to class, take the stairs. Get slightly out of breath.

Fuel

Keep week 1 going. Add: one plant (fruit, veg, beans, nuts) to every meal, and swap one sugary or energy drink a day for water.

Recover

Keep weeks 1–2. Add: a consistent sleep window on at least 5 nights, and screens down 30 minutes before bed.

Lock in

All three together. Plus one session of movement with a friend — a walk, a gym trip, a kickabout. Social habits stick harder.

Before you start

Quick questions

Do I need to be fit already?

No. The whole point is a low floor. If 10 minutes of brisk walking is a lot right now, that's a perfect week 1.

What if I miss a day?

Nothing happens. Don't try to "make it up" — just tick the next day. Streaks are a bonus, not the goal.

Is this safe for everyone?

For most young adults, light-to-moderate movement and eating more plants is safe. If you have a diagnosed heart condition, are pregnant, or get chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness with exercise, check with a doctor first.
